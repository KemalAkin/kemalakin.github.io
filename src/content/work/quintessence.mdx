An alternative to the standard model of cosmology ($\Lambda\mathrm{CDM}$) is built from scalar-field models of
dark energy. The stability of radiation–matter–quintessence models is analysed with symbolic methods (SymPy and
Mathematica) and numerical ones (NumPy and SciPy), and the trajectories in phase space are visualized with
Matplotlib.

The graduation project at Istanbul Technical University: *Dynamical system analysis of quintessence models*.
The [ΛCDM note](/writing/dynamical-system-lcdm/) works through the simplest case.
