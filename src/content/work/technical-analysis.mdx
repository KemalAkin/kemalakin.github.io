Technical analysis of stock and crypto markets. The toolkit pulls candlestick data through exchange APIs,
works on it with Pandas and technical-analysis libraries, and plots candlestick charts overlaid with indicators
(RSI, MACD, moving averages) using Matplotlib, Seaborn and Plotly. The same charts are available on request
through a Telegram bot.
