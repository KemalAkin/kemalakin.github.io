---
title: Crypto portfolio tracker
summary: Transaction history from exchange APIs turned into profit and loss, average buy price and cost per asset, served through a Telegram bot.
stack: [Python, Exchange APIs, Telegram]
order: 8
---

The tracker reads the transaction history of an account through the broker's API (Binance and FTX at the time)
and calculates profit and loss, the average buy price and the cost of each asset. Users reach their portfolio
through a Telegram bot by providing their own API key.
