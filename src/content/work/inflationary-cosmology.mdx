The M.Sc. thesis at Istanbul Technical University: *Theoretical and observational aspects of inflationary
cosmology*.

The theory of the early universe is built up from first principles. Model-independent observational constraints
are analysed with Bayesian inference in CosmoMC, and inflaton models within and beyond general relativity are
tested against the [Planck](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/planck) data sets.
