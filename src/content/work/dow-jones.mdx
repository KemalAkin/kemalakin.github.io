The *Dow Jones Industrial Average* is a stock market index of 30 prominent companies listed on US exchanges. In
this project I collect data on the companies in the index by web scraping (Pandas and Yahoo Finance), clean it
and build a database with Python. Interactive dashboards on top of it are built in Microsoft Power BI and
Tableau.
