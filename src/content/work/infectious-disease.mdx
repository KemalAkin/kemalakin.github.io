The SIR (susceptible, infectious, recovered) model is derived analytically and its coupled differential
equations are solved both with SciPy's integrators and with a manual fourth-order Runge–Kutta implementation.
The stability of the disease-free and endemic states is determined symbolically with SymPy, and the evolution of
the disease through a population is visualized with Matplotlib.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{dS}{dt} &= -\frac{\beta}{N} S I \\[1ex]
\frac{dI}{dt} &= \frac{\beta}{N} S I - \gamma I \\[1ex]
\frac{dR}{dt} &= \gamma I
\end{aligned}
$$

Here $\beta$ is the infection rate, $\gamma$ the recovery rate and $N = S + I + R$ the population size. The
[interactive note](/writing/sir-model/) puts $\beta$ and $\gamma$ on sliders.
