---
title: Classification of forensic evidence
summary: Physical and chemical analyses of document, ballistic and biological evidence classified with PCA.
year: 2019–2021
stack: [scikit-learn, PCA]
order: 9
---

Physical and chemical analyses of forensic evidence, from document forgery and ballistics to biological
materials, were classified with principal component analysis (PCA) in scikit-learn. A local database for each
class of evidence was built so that future evidence could be matched against it.

The studies and their data are confidential, so no output from the original work is shown here. For academic
interest, feel free to get in touch.
